# Ofwat PR24 Final Determinations — Storm Overflows & AMP8 ODIs

## [OFWAT-PR24-ODI-01] AMP8 Storm Overflow Performance Commitment & ODI Penalties
Under Ofwat PR24 Final Determinations (2025-2030 AMP8), water and sewerage companies face Outcome Delivery Incentive (ODI) penalties for storm overflow spills exceeding company-specific annual targets (benchmark <= 20 spills per overflow per year toward the 40% reduction milestone by 2030). Standard ODI underperformance rates apply per excess spill (£5,500 standard / £11,000 sensitive site) plus £1,250 per excess spill hour, escalating for repeated failures.

## [OFWAT-PR24-PCD-02] Price Control Deliverable (PCD) Clawback & Pollution Incidents
Ofwat PR24 imposes severe ODI penalties for Category 1-3 serious pollution incidents and mandates automatic customer bill clawbacks where storm overflow capital enhancement schemes fail to deliver contracted storage, separation, or green-infrastructure spill reductions.

## [OFWAT-PR24-STORM-03] PR24 Spill Reduction & Nature-Based Solutions Delivery
Water companies must report audited annual EDM spill counts, total spill duration hours, and catchment-level ecological risk reduction across AMP8 (2025-2030), prioritizing sustainable drainage systems (SuDS) and storage upgrades on high-frequency CSOs.
