# Environment Act 2021 — Storm Overflow Reduction & Continuous Monitoring Duties

## [ENV-ACT-2021-S141A-01] Section 141A Storm Overflow Discharge Reduction Plan
Section 141A of the Environment Act 2021 places a statutory duty on sewerage undertakers in England to progressively reduce adverse ecological and public health impacts from storm overflows: 75% reduction of discharges into high-priority sites (designated Bathing Waters, Sites of Special Scientific Interest, and chalk streams) by 2035, and 100% elimination of ecological harm across all storm overflows by 2050.

## [ENV-ACT-2021-S141B-02] Sections 141B & 141C Near-Real-Time EDM & Water Quality Reporting
Sections 141B and 141C require sewerage undertakers to publish Event Duration Monitoring (EDM) spill start and stop notifications within 1 hour of discharge onset and maintain continuous upstream and downstream dissolved oxygen, turbidity, temperature, pH, and ammonia monitoring.
