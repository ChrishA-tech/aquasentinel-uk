# Environment Agency Event Duration Monitoring (EDM) Permitting Rules

## [EA-EDM-PERMIT-01] Dry-Weather Overflow Prohibition & Permit Breach Enforcement
Environment Agency environmental permits strictly prohibit Combined Sewer Overflow (CSO) discharges during dry weather; overflows are permitted only in response to rainfall or snowmelt exceeding sewer capacity. A spill occurring when 24-hour antecedent rainfall is <= 0.25 mm and Antecedent Precipitation Index (API) is <= 2.0 mm constitutes a suspected dry-day spill permit breach subject to EA investigation and unlimited fines.

## [EA-EDM-DATA-02] 15-Minute Telemetry Completeness & Flatlined Sensor Quarantine
EA EDM permitting guidance requires >= 90% telemetry operational completeness (rising to 98% under PR24 best practice) and applies the 12/24-hour block spill counting methodology. Stuck or flatlined level and dissolved oxygen sensors must be quarantined immediately into dead-letter storage and replaced by validated spatial interpolation or physical inspection records.
